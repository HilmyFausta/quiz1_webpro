Profile

Kare Rajungan

Rajungan curry is a traditional curry dish made with thick coconut milk and slices of rajungan, small blue crabs with sweet, fresh meat. The broth has a rich, savory, and slightly spicy flavor that pairs perfectly with warm rice. With its appetizing aroma of spices, this dish is a top choice and highly recommended for anyone who loves seafood.

Legen

Legen is a traditional beverage with natural sweetness, tapped directly from the sap of the siwalan (lontar) palm flower. With a light, refreshing, and slightly tart flavor, this drink is best enjoyed cold on a hot afternoon. Its natural freshness makes it the perfect thirst-quencher and is deeply associated with life in coastal communities.

Ampo

Ampo is a unique traditional snack made from select clay that has been cleaned and baked until it becomes crispy and develops a distinctive aroma. This snack offers an unusual savory flavor, making it one of the most iconic and rare culinary delights, one that is very hard to find outside the Tuban region.

Rujak Engos-Engos

A traditional Tuban coastal rujak made with fresh seaweed (engos-engos) and vegetables, drizzled with a spicy, salty, and savory sauce. The chewy texture of the seaweed delivers an authentic taste of the sea. Served with a choice of petis or peanut sauce, this dish is the perfect snack for anyone who loves fresh seafood.

Rica-rica Belut

Rica-rica Belut is a legendary dish prepared with an abundance of spices, resulting in a chewy texture and a uniquely spicy and savory flavor. It differs from typical eel dishes, such as rica-rica, peanut-sauced satay, and the refreshing rujak sauce. Served warm with rice and es legen, this dish is a must-try culinary experience highly recommended for spicy food lovers.

Becek Menthok

Becek menthok is a legendary dish from Tuban made with Manila duck cooked in a thick, spicy coconut milk broth rich in fresh spices. Unlike most gulai dishes, this dish features the strong aromas of onions, chili peppers, ginger, and galangal, without the use of cloves. Served warm with white rice or corn rice, becek menthok is a truly authentic and filling choice for breakfast or lunch.
