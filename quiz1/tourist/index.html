Profile

Pantai Kelapa

A beach lined with coconut trees along its shore, a good spot to relax and take in the sea breeze. It offers a quieter side of Tuban's coastline compared to the busier parts of town.

Sunan Bonang Religious Site

The tomb complex of Sunan Bonang, one of the saints who helped spread Islam across Java. The site draws pilgrims from many regions and is one of the reasons Tuban carries the nickname Kota Wali.
